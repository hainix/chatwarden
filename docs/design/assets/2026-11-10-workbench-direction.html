One case per person

Reports about the same player merge into one case. Claiming the case covers all of them, so two moderators never decide the same player at once.

Take next, or pick one

"Take next" hands you the oldest unclaimed case. Opening a case claims it. Others see who has it. A senior can take a case over.

Nothing waits on a spinner

The queue refreshes in the background. New reports appear behind a pill, so the list never jumps under your cursor. A decision leaves your screen at once, with a few seconds to undo.

Easier on the people doing it

Blocked words are hidden until you choose to see them. The suggested step, the rule and the lines to show the player come pre-filled. Saved reasons cut the typing.
